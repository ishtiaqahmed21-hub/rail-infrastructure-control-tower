Cost & Earned Value

PV=sum(BAC*planned fraction); EV=sum(BAC*earned physical fraction); AC=sum(cost); CPI=EV/AC; SPI=EV/PV; SV=EV-PV and CV=EV-AC are currency. EAC=AC+(BAC-EV)/CPI, with AC+unpaid commitments as floor. Remaining commitments cover unspent obligations and are not added to EAC again. No contingency is embedded; risk exposure is reported separately and must not be added mechanically because risks may overlap.
